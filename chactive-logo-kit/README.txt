CHACTIVE LOGO KIT

Concept: Bubble C. The letter C drawn as a chat bubble with a tail, and a gold dot in its opening like an online status light.

COLORS
Emerald   #00C170  (on light backgrounds use #00A862)
Gold      #FFB020  (on light backgrounds use #E59A00)
Ink       #0B0F14
Off-white #F2F5F7

FOLDERS
svg/    Vector files. Use these whenever possible.
png/    Ready-to-use PNGs: icon sizes 1024 to 16, colorways, symbol only, lockups.
store/  Square full-bleed icons for the App Store (1024) and Google Play (512). The stores round the corners themselves.

NOTES
- Lockup SVGs use live text set in Plus Jakarta Sans (free on Google Fonts). Install it, or the text falls back to another sans-serif.
- Lockup PNGs use a stand-in typeface until the final brand font is chosen.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to about a quarter of the icon width.
- Do not stretch the logo or recolor it outside the colorways provided.
